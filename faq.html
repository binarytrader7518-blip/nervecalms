What is NerveCalm?

NerveCalm is a botanical dietary supplement in capsule form. The seller formulates it for adults who want daily support for nerve comfort, and it combines Corydalis yanhusuo, Opuntia phaeacantha, marshmallow root and California poppy. Read the full NerveCalm product overview.

What ingredients are in NerveCalm?

The seller names Corydalis yanhusuo, Opuntia phaeacantha, marshmallow root (Althaea officinalis) and California poppy seed. The front of the bottle also lists passion flower. The seller does not publish a per-ingredient milligram breakdown, so check the Supplement Facts panel on your bottle. See ingredient details.

How do I take NerveCalm?

According to the seller, take two capsules once a day with a meal and a glass of water, ideally in the morning. If you take prescription medication, the seller advises spacing NerveCalm 30 minutes before or after it, and checking with your doctor or pharmacist first. More in How It Works.

How long does one bottle last?

Each bottle holds 60 capsules. At two capsules a day, that is a 30-day supply. The 3-bottle package covers 90 days and the 6-bottle package covers 180 days.

How long before I notice anything?

Individual experiences vary. The seller says some customers notice changes in mood, sleep or energy within about a week, while fuller nerve-support results usually take three to six months. It recommends using NerveCalm consistently for at least four to six months.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes. The seller offers a 90-day money-back guarantee and says refunds are handled by contacting its support team by email. Review the seller's refund terms at checkout before you order.

Are there any side effects?

The seller says no significant side effects have been reported for its ingredients. Herbal ingredients can still interact with medications, and California poppy has been studied for effects on drug-metabolizing enzymes. Speak with your doctor first if you take medication, are pregnant or nursing, or have a health condition. See our guide to supplements and medications.
