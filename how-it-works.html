Directions

How to Take NerveCalm

These directions come from the seller. Never take more than the label recommends.

Take two capsules daily

Take two capsules once a day with a meal and a glass of water, ideally in the morning.

Separate from medication

If you take prescription medication, take NerveCalm 30 minutes before or after it, and ask your doctor or pharmacist first.

Give it time

The seller says some people notice changes in mood, sleep or energy within about a week, with fuller results usually taking 3 to 6 months.
