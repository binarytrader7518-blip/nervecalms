Inside the bottle

NerveCalm Ingredients & Product Details

These are the botanicals the seller names for NerveCalm, along with the key product specifications.

Corydalis yanhusuo

A flowering plant whose tuber has a long history in traditional Chinese herbal practice. Modern lab and animal research focuses on its alkaloids, which researchers are studying for their role in how the body processes discomfort.

Opuntia phaeacantha

A prickly pear cactus native to the American Southwest. The Opuntia family is studied for its polyphenols and other plant antioxidants.

Marshmallow Root (Althaea officinalis)

The root of Althaea officinalis, a traditional herb best known for its soothing mucilage. The seller lists it under both its common and botanical names.

California Poppy

Eschscholzia californica, the state flower of California. It has a traditional European use for relieving mild symptoms of mental stress and supporting sleep.
