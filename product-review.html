NerveCalm Review: Is It Worth Trying?

Short answer: NerveCalm is a botanical nerve-comfort capsule with simple directions, one-time pricing and a 90-day money-back guarantee. It is best suited to adults who want a plant-based addition to their routine and are willing to be consistent.

What NerveCalm is

NerveCalm is a dietary supplement sold in bottles of 60 capsules. The seller names Corydalis yanhusuo, Opuntia phaeacantha, marshmallow root and California poppy as its ingredients, and the front of the bottle also lists passion flower. The directions are two capsules a day with a meal. Full product overview →

Seller claims vs. what we can verify

  • Verified from the seller's page: capsule count and directions, the named ingredients, package prices, shipping terms, the 90-day guarantee, and one-time pricing.
  • Seller claims we cannot verify: the 4.8/5 rating from 20,883 reviews, "most bioavailable" ingredient forms, and the results timelines.
  • Not available: clinical trials on NerveCalm as a finished product, and per-ingredient dosages.

Ingredient quality

The botanicals have genuine traditional histories, and some have been studied in research. Corydalis alkaloids, for example, appear in peer-reviewed reviews. Most of that research is preclinical, though, and it doesn't prove the finished formula works. See our scientific references.

Our verdict

NerveCalm is a reasonable option for adults who want to try a botanical nerve-comfort supplement and can commit to several months of daily use. The guarantee reduces the financial risk. If you take medication or have a diagnosed condition, talk to your doctor first.
